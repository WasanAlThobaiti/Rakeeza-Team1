Follow-up action: Repeat the measurement over a longer production-like workload. If the TTFT alert fires, check recent traffic, vLLM latency metrics, service health, and resource pressure.

## Measurement query

```promql
histogram_quantile(
  0.95,
  sum by (le) (
    rate(vllm:time_to_first_token_seconds_bucket{job="serving"}[5m])
  )
)
```

The expression calculates the p95 time to first token from the vLLM TTFT histogram over a trailing 5-minute Prometheus window. It was evaluated during the artificial traffic workload on 2026-09-14. The measurement is taken from the vLLM serving job and measures first-token latency. It does not measure model-output correctness, response quality, or user satisfaction.

## Service alert

Condition and unit: p95 TTFT > 0.10 seconds.

Evaluation interval: 30 seconds.

Pending period: 1 minute.

Relationship to the SLO: The alert condition detects when p95 TTFT exceeds the SLO target of 0.10 seconds. A sustained value above this threshold indicates that users are waiting longer than the target for the first generated token.

First response to a notification: Check recent traffic and vLLM TTFT latency metrics, then inspect service health and resource pressure.
